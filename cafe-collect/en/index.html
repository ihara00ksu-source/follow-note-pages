Places you visit, collected one card at a time.

Cafe Collect is an iPhone app for collecting photos and memories of places as cards. Your card information stays on your device.

Keep up to 30 cards for free. The one-time Cafe Collect Plus purchase adds kraft paper, stainless steel, and glass materials and removes the card-count limit. Each card can hold up to five photos.
