1. Scope and operator

This policy explains how TSUYOSHI IHARA (the “Operator”) handles information in Cafe Collect (the “App”), on its official support website, and when responding to support inquiries.

Contact: pon.apps.support@gmail.com

2. Information used in the App

The App uses the place name, address, area, visit date, visit history, rating, note, favorites, and photos you choose to enter (up to five photos per card). To display, edit, and organize this information, it also stores card and visit identifiers, card creation and update dates, visit creation dates, photo storage locations, and the order of photos and cards.

Preferences, such as your selected card material, are stored on your device so the same appearance can be used next time you open the App. This information is used to create, display, edit, search, filter, and reorder cards, and to create a sharing video of a card you select.

The App does not require account registration, provide its own sign-in system, access your contacts, or obtain your current location through GPS or similar features. You enter addresses and areas yourself.

3. Storage and sharing outside the device

Card information and photos are stored in the App’s dedicated storage on your device, and preferences are also stored on the device. The App has no feature that sends card contents to the Operator’s servers or lets the Operator view them. It does not provide its own cloud synchronization.

The sharing feature creates a short video on your device showing the selected card’s photo, place name, address, visit date, rating, note, and other displayed details. Only when you choose to share does the App pass the video to an app you select in the iOS share sheet. It does not automatically post to social media or upload to the Operator’s servers. Storage, publication, and transmission by a receiving service are governed by that service’s settings, terms, and privacy policy.

The App does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, or its own crash-reporting SDK. It does not track you for advertising. Demo photos and fonts are bundled with the App and are not fetched from external websites for display.

In-app purchases, the operating system’s photo selection, saving, backup, and diagnostic features, the apps you choose to share with, email, and visits to the official website may involve processing by the external services described below.

4. Photo library

The App reads photos you select through the operating system’s photo picker and copies them to its dedicated storage for use on cards. It does not collect or transmit your entire photo library, or change or delete your original photos. You can also create cards without photos.

If a photo you select contains metadata such as its capture date or location, that metadata may remain in the imported photo file. The App does not use that location metadata to identify or classify places.

Selecting photos stored in iCloud or similar services may cause the operating system or photo provider to connect to the network to retrieve them. You can change photo access permissions in iOS Settings. To delete a copy already imported into the App, remove the photo while editing its card.

When you select “Save to Photos,” the App requests permission to add the sharing video to your photo library. It does not request permission to read your entire library for this action. You can delete a saved video in Photos. If you use iCloud Photos or similar services, the video may be synchronized according to your operating system settings.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases and purchase restoration use Apple’s App Store and StoreKit. To confirm access to paid features, the App processes product identifiers, purchase status, verified transaction information, and related information supplied by Apple on your device. It has no mechanism to send this information to the Operator’s own servers.

Apple handles payment methods, payment information, and Apple Account credentials. Neither the App nor the Operator obtains your credit card number or Apple Account password. The Operator may view aggregate information, such as sales totals, in Apple’s management tools.

Apple’s handling of information is governed by its privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

6. Operating system backups and diagnostics

Depending on your device and operating system settings, App data may be included in operating system backups, such as iCloud backups, or in data transfers when changing devices. These are not synchronization or backup services operated by the App. The settings and terms of the operating system and services you use apply.

Depending on your operating system’s diagnostic and analytics sharing settings, Apple or other platform providers may process diagnostic information. The App does not independently transmit diagnostic data.

7. Retention and deletion

Card information remains on your device until you delete the card. Visit entries can be deleted individually; deleting a place’s card also deletes its visit history. Removing a photo from a card or deleting the card deletes its in-app photo copy if no other card refers to it. Original photos in your photo library are not deleted.

Temporary files used to create sharing videos are deleted after closing the sharing screen or during related cleanup. Deleting a card does not delete videos saved in Photos or passed to another app. Delete those copies from the relevant destination if needed.

To remove the App’s dedicated data altogether, use “Delete App” in iOS. “Offload App” keeps the data. Operating system backups and original photos are stored separately; delete them from their respective locations if needed.

The Operator does not store your card information or photos and cannot recover data lost through deletion, device failure, or similar events. Deleting the App does not immediately delete purchase records managed by Apple. Contact Apple about its handling of purchase records.

8. Support inquiries

If you contact us by email, we use your email address, name (if provided), message, and attachments as needed to respond, investigate issues, and provide follow-up support. You are not required to send an email to use paid features you have purchased.

Inquiry information is retained as long as needed to handle your request. Information no longer needed after the request is resolved is deleted, unless retention is required by law or necessary to address a dispute. The email service provider processes the transmission, receipt, and storage of email.

Please do not send personal information from your cards, your entire photo library, your Apple Account password, or payment details. Requests concerning access to, correction of, or deletion of inquiry information can be sent to the contact listed below. We will respond in accordance with applicable law and may verify your identity when necessary.

9. Official support website

When you open the official support website in a browser, its hosting provider, GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), may process IP addresses, request information, and similar data to deliver and protect the pages. Information stored in your cards is not included with these requests.

The official support website does not contain the Operator’s own analytics, advertising, or input forms. For GitHub’s handling of information, see: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

The browser preview used during development differs from the released App and involves authentication and access processing by its hosting provider. Records entered in the preview are kept only in memory and disappear when the page is reloaded. The preview does not support actual purchases or exporting sharing videos.

10. Disclosure and safeguards

The Operator does not sell support inquiry information or disclose it to third parties for advertising. Information is not disclosed to third parties except with your consent, when processing is entrusted to service providers as necessary to handle your request, when required by law, or in other legally permitted circumstances.

The Operator manages information within the scope needed for its purpose and takes steps to prevent unnecessary access. To protect cards stored on your device, please also use operating system security features such as a device passcode.

11. Changes and contact

This policy will be revised if features or information-handling practices change. Material changes will be announced in the App or on the official website before they take effect, and consent will be obtained when required by law.
