2. What the App provides

The App lets you record places you have visited as cards on your device, with photos, place names, addresses, areas, visit dates, ratings, and notes, and browse and organize those cards. You can record places other than cafes.

You can create a short video showing the front and back of a card, pass it to another app using the iOS share sheet, or save it to Photos. The App does not provide an in-app social network, a review-posting service, account registration, or its own cloud synchronization. It does not guarantee the accuracy of recorded place information or whether a place is currently operating.

Sharing videos include card contents such as notes and addresses. Check the preview before sharing. The receiving service’s settings and terms govern its conditions of use and the audience for your post. Some apps may not accept videos directly; in that case, save the video to Photos and select it from the receiving app.

3. Free and paid features

The free version lets you store up to 30 cards and use the standard paper material. Both free and paid versions allow up to five photos per card. If you delete cards and have fewer than 30 stored cards, you can add new ones.

The one-time purchase “Cafe Collect Plus” includes three card materials—kraft paper, stainless steel, and glass—and removes the card-count limit. Materials change the on-screen appearance; no physical cards or materials are sold.

“Unlimited” means removing the App’s 30-card limit. Storage and performance remain subject to available device space, operating system and hardware performance, and similar constraints.
