How many cards can I add for free?

You can store up to 30 cards. The limit counts cards, not photos, and applies to all stored cards even when you filter by area or favorites. Each card can contain up to five photos.

At 30 cards, you can still view, edit, and delete existing cards. Delete cards to bring the count below 30, and you can add new ones again.

What is included in Cafe Collect Plus?

Plus includes three card materials—kraft paper, stainless steel, and glass—and removes the card-count limit. It is a one-time purchase with no automatic renewal or monthly fee. Materials change the appearance of cards. The limit of five photos per card also applies to Plus.

Available device storage and similar constraints still apply after the card-count limit is removed. The purchase price is shown on the App Store confirmation screen before you buy.

I purchased Plus but cannot use it

Check your network connection and that you are using the App Store with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Then select “Restore purchases” in Cafe Collect Settings. If a purchase is awaiting parental or other approval, paid features will become available after approval.

If the issue continues, contact us with the App and iOS versions, what appeared on screen, and when it happened. Do not send your Apple Account password or credit card number.

Does restoring purchases also restore my cards?

“Restore purchases” restores access to paid features. Card photos and notes are stored on your device and are not restored by this action.

The App does not provide its own cloud synchronization or data backup. Cards cannot be restored from sharing videos. When changing devices, check the requirements for iOS data migration and backups. Keep the App on your original device until you have confirmed the data on the new one.

How do I request a refund?

Request a refund for an App Store purchase through Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com/ . Eligibility depends on Apple’s rules and applicable law.

If your entitlement is revoked, stored cards are not automatically deleted. You can still view, edit, and delete them. The free limit of 30 cards and the standard paper material apply again.

How do I edit cards and photos?

Tap a card to lift it, then select “Edit.” The first photo appears on the front. Tap a small photo thumbnail to replace or remove it. Removing the first photo makes the next photo the front image.

Tap the photo count on the back of a card to browse its photos by swiping left or right. You can create a card with just a place name and no photo.

How do I reorder cards?

On the card stack screen, touch and hold a card, then drag it left or right. The order is saved when you release it. When an area or other filter is active, the order changes among the visible cards.

What happens if I delete the App?

“Delete App” in iOS also deletes its dedicated data on the device. “Offload App” keeps the data. Original photos in your library and operating system backups are managed separately.

The Operator does not keep copies of your cards and cannot recover lost cards. A valid one-time purchase can be restored using “Restore purchases” with the same Apple Account.

About the browser preview

The browser preview is for checking the design and interactions. Added or edited records disappear when the page is reloaded. Actual purchases and sharing-video exports are not available. The sharing screen shows a preview of the card flip. This differs from video creation and saving, persistent on-device storage, and App Store purchases and restoration in the iPhone app.

How do I record visits with stamps?

Tap a card to lift it, then open “Visits.” Choose a date and tap “Add a stamp” to record it in that place’s stamp book. Each spread shows six visits; switch pages to view earlier entries.

Tap a stamp to correct its date. You can also touch and hold it to choose date correction or deletion. Correcting a date does not increase the visit count. The visit date entered when creating the card stays unchanged and does not automatically become a stamp. You can record multiple visits on the same day. Visit history is available for free and stored on your device.

How do I share a card on X or Instagram?

Tap a card to lift it, then tap the share icon to the right of “Visits.” The App creates an approximately five-second silent video flipping between the front and back, using the selected material and including your note. Check the preview, then tap “Share” to open the iOS share sheet and select a destination.

Whether X or Instagram appears as a destination, and which posting screen opens, depends on the apps installed on your device and their support for sharing. If direct sharing is unavailable, choose “Save to Photos,” then select that video in X or Instagram. If you denied permission to add to your photo library, you can change it in iOS Settings.

Card contents are not published automatically. Videos include the address and note, so check the video and the destination’s audience settings before sending. Long text is shown only to the extent that it fits on the video card. Sharing is also available for free.
